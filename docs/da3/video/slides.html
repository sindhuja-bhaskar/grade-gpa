Q2 (d) · Threat agents and regulation

Two threat agents, two controls, GDPR

Threat agentControl implementedGDPR requirement
External attacker
reusing stolen or phished passwords		TOTP MFA (single-use codes, encrypted secret) + salted bcrypt + rate limitArt. 32: security appropriate to the risk, incl. encryption and confidentiality
Malicious insider
a peer or staff member altering marks		Read-only ADVISOR role (403), ownership checks, revocable sessions, non-root containersArt. 5(1)(f) integrity and confidentiality; Art. 25 data protection by design

GDPR applies because names, emails and marks are personal data of identifiable students. India's DPDP Act 2023 sets the same duty.
